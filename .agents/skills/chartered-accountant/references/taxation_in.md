# Indian Taxation Guide: Income Tax, TDS, GST & Capital Gains

This reference document outlines the statutory tax framework under the Income Tax Act, 1961 (amended as per latest Finance Act updates) and Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017.

---

## 1. Income Tax Slabs for Individuals (Assessment Year 2025–26)

### Default: New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC)
| Taxable Income Slab | Tax Rate | Cumulative Tax at Slab Top |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹3,00,000 | **Nil** | ₹0 |
| ₹3,00,001 – ₹7,00,000 | **5%** | ₹20,000 |
| ₹7,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | **10%** | ₹50,000 |
| ₹10,00,001 – ₹12,00,000 | **15%** | ₹80,000 |
| ₹12,00,001 – ₹15,00,000 | **20%** | ₹1,40,000 |
| Above ₹15,00,000 | **30%** | ₹1,40,000 + 30% of income above ₹15L |

#### Key Provisions for New Regime:
- **Standard Deduction**: ₹75,000 for salaried individuals and pensioners.
- **Section 87A Rebate**: Full rebate if taxable income $\le$ ₹7,00,000. Effectively, salaried individuals with Gross Total Income up to **₹7,75,000 pay zero tax**.
- **Family Pension Deduction**: Standard deduction of ₹25,000 or 1/3rd of pension (whichever is lower).
- **Health & Education Cess**: 4% mandatory on total income tax.

### Optional: Old Tax Regime
| Taxable Income Slab | Tax Rate |
| :--- | :--- |
| Up to ₹2,50,000 | **Nil** |
| ₹2,50,001 – ₹5,00,000 | **5%** |
| ₹5,00,001 – ₹10,00,000 | **20%** |
| Above ₹10,00,000 | **30%** |

#### Chapter VI-A Deductions Allowed in Old Regime:
1. **Section 80C**: Up to ₹1,50,000 (EPF, PPF, ELSS, Life Insurance, Principal on Home Loan, SSY, NSC).
2. **Section 80D**: Health insurance premium:
   - Self/family: ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if senior citizen).
   - Parents: ₹25,000 (₹50,000 if senior citizen).
   - Preventive health checkup: ₹5,000 within total limit.
3. **Section 80CCD(1B)**: Additional NPS contribution up to ₹50,000.
4. **Section 24(b)**: Interest on home loan (self-occupied property) up to ₹2,00,000.
5. **HRA Exemption (Section 10(13A))**: Minimum of:
   - Actual HRA received
   - 50% (metro) or 40% (non-metro) of basic salary
   - Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary
6. **Section 87A Rebate**: Up to ₹12,500 if taxable income does not exceed ₹5,00,000.

## 2. Capital Gains Tax Structure

| Asset Class | Holding Period | STCG (Short-Term) | LTCG (Long-Term) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Listed Equities & Equity Mutual Funds** | $\le 12$ months (ST) / $> 12$ months (LT) | **20%** (u/s 111A) | **12.5%** (u/s 112A) over ₹1.25 Lakh exemption |
| **Listed Bonds, Debentures, G-Secs** | $\le 12$ months / $> 12$ months | Slab Rate | **12.5%** (without indexation) |
| **Real Estate (Immovable Property)** | $\le 24$ months / $> 24$ months | Slab Rate | **12.5%** (without indexation) |
| **Gold & Physical Precious Metals** | $\le 24$ months / $> 24$ months | Slab Rate | **12.5%** (without indexation) |
| **Debt Mutual Funds (acquired on/after Apr 1, 2023)** | Any holding period | Slab Rate | Slab Rate (treated as short-term u/s 50AA) |

## 3. Advance Tax Schedule (Section 208–211)

Applicable if estimated net tax liability (after TDS) exceeds ₹10,000 in a financial year:
- **On or before June 15**: Minimum 15% of total tax liability
- **On or before September 15**: Minimum 45% of total tax liability
- **On or before December 15**: Minimum 75% of total tax liability
- **On or before March 15**: 100% of total tax liability
- *Interest for default*: Section 234B (shortfall in advance tax) and Section 234C (deferment of installments) @ 1% simple interest per month.

## 5. Goods and Services Tax (GST) Quick Reference

### Filing Requirements
- **GSTR-1**: Details of outward supplies of goods and services (Monthly or Quarterly under QRMP).
- **GSTR-2B**: Static auto-drafted ITC statement based on suppliers' GSTR-1. Crucial for matching input credit.
- **GSTR-3B**: Monthly summary return with tax payment.
- **GSTR-9**: Annual consolidated return.

### Input Tax Credit (ITC) Rules (Rule 36(4) / Section 16)
1. Recipient must possess a valid tax invoice or debit note.
2. The supplier must have reported the invoice in their GSTR-1 and it must reflect in the buyer's GSTR-2B.
3. The supplier must have paid the tax to the Government.
4. Goods or services must have been received.
5. Ineligible ITC (Section 17(5) - Blocked Credits): Motor vehicles for personal transport, food & beverages, outdoor catering, membership of clubs/gyms (unless statutory requirement).
