Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 • Version 1.2

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At Financy, your privacy and financial security are our foundational principles. We do not sell your personal or financial data to advertisers, brokers, or third parties under any circumstances. You own your financial data.

2. Data We Collect & How We Use It

  • Account Information: When you register via Sign in with Apple, Google OAuth, or Email OTP, we store your authenticated user identifier and email address (or Apple Private Relay anonymous proxy).
  • Financial Records: Transaction entries, budget allocations, recurring schedules, and accounts you manually enter or categorize are stored in your private encrypted database partition.
  • Device & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash diagnostics to improve iOS stability and app performance.

3. Apple Sign-In & On-Device Security

Financy integrates natively with Apple's AuthenticationServices framework. Biometric authentication (Face ID & Touch ID) executes purely on-device via Apple's Secure Enclave. We never access your biometric templates or raw credentials.

4. Encryption & Cloud Storage

All client-server communications are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 / HTTPS. Cloud databases enforce Row Level Security (RLS) in PostgreSQL, guaranteeing that only your verified JWT session can access your data records.

5. Account Deletion & Data Rights

In compliance with Apple App Store review guidelines and global privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA), you can delete your account and all associated financial records directly from the iOS app settings (More > Account > Delete Account) at any time.

6. Contact Us

For questions regarding this policy or privacy requests, please reach out to privacy@financy.app.
